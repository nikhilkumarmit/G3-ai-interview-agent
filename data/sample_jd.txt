Role: Senior Backend Engineer - Distributed Systems
Company: FinTech Velocity Systems
Location: Remote / San Francisco, CA

About the Role:
We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Core Banking Infrastructure team. You will be responsible for architecting and building high-throughput payment settlement pipelines, event streaming services, and resilient distributed workflows.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, implement, and maintain low-latency backend microservices using Python and Go.
- Build resilient event-driven architectures with Apache Kafka or Redis Streams capable of processing financial transactions with strict idempotency.
- Optimize relational databases (PostgreSQL) and caching systems for high concurrency and zero data loss.
- Collaborate cross-functionally with Product, Security, and DevOps to deliver reliable, observable software.
- Drive engineering excellence through code reviews, architectural RFCs, and mentoring team members.

Required Qualifications:
- 3+ years of professional backend engineering experience with Python or Go.
- Proven hands-on experience building event-driven systems with Kafka, RabbitMQ, or Redis streams.
- Strong proficiency in SQL, PostgreSQL performance tuning, and database migration strategies.
- Deep understanding of distributed systems principles, concurrency, and REST/gRPC API design.
- Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud platforms (AWS/GCP).
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.

Nice to Have:
- Experience in FinTech, banking transaction processing, or payment gateways.
- Familiarity with CI/CD automation and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform).
- Contributions to open-source distributed computing or async libraries.
